🌊 Sverdrup Balance · 位涡平衡

Wind-driven ocean circulation and the vorticity balance · 风生大洋环流与位涡平衡机制

📊 Sverdrup Balance Diagram · 位涡平衡示意图

The classic schematic of wind-driven gyres, Ekman dynamics, and the vorticity response. · 风生环流、埃克曼动力学与位涡响应的经典示意图。

🔑 Key Concepts · 核心概念

① Wind causes Ekman transport and convergence · 风驱动埃克曼输送与辐合
西风带（Westerlies）与信风带（Trades）驱动表层埃克曼输送，导致大洋中部产生 Ekman 泵送（pumping）或抽吸（suction）。
The Westerlies and Trade Winds drive surface Ekman transport, causing Ekman pumping or suction in the central ocean.

② Ekman pumping provides squashing or stretching · 埃克曼泵送导致水柱压缩或拉伸
辐合的 Ekman 输送将水柱压扁（squashing），辐散则将其拉伸（stretching）。这一垂直运动改变了水柱的相对涡度。
Convergent Ekman transport squashes the water column; divergent transport stretches it. This vertical motion alters the relative vorticity of the column.

③ Vorticity response: change latitude, not spin up · 位涡响应：改变纬度而非原地加速
水柱必须通过改变相对涡度或行星涡度（即迁移纬度）来响应，而不是原地旋转加速。这是因为位涡守恒：
Water columns must respond by changing relative vorticity or planetary vorticity (i.e., migrating in latitude), rather than spinning up in place — a consequence of potential-vorticity conservation:

(f + ζ) / h = constant  ⇒  Squashing (h↓) → equatorward movement (f↓) · 压缩 → 向赤道移动
(f + ζ) / h = constant  ⇒  Stretching (h↑) → poleward movement (f↑) · 拉伸 → 向极地移动

Sverdrup transport · 斯文德鲁普输送：风生环流（Gyres）中，Ekman 泵送/抽吸驱动的经向体积输送，平衡了风应力旋度的作用。
Within the wind-driven gyres, the meridional volume transport driven by Ekman pumping/suction balances the wind-stress curl.

💡 记忆口诀 · Memory aid：Squashing → 水柱变扁 → 为保持位涡守恒 → f 必须减小 → 向赤道移动；Stretching → 水柱变长 → f 必须增大 → 向极地移动。 Squashing flattens the column → to conserve PV, f must decrease → move equatorward; Stretching lengthens it → f must increase → move poleward.

🧪 Worked Example · Geostrophic Flow across the Gulf Stream · 地转流计算范例

Density section → dynamic height → geostrophic velocity, step by step. · 由密度断面推求动力高度与地转流速，逐步推演。

Question · 题目：Given the density section across the Gulf Stream (station A at 38.2°N, station B at 39.2°N, L ≈ 100 km apart), find the dynamic-height difference and the geostrophic velocity, and determine the flow direction.
已知墨西哥湾流断面的密度分布（测站 A 位于 38.2°N，测站 B 位于 39.2°N，相距 L ≈ 100 km），求动力高度差与地转流速，并判断流向。

图注 · Legend：(a) 位势密度断面 σθ (kg/m³)：红色为低密度暖水（南侧），绿色为高密度冷水（北侧），黑色三角为陆坡地形；(b) 地转流速剖面 v(z) (cm/s)，红色虚线为参考面。 (a) Potential-density section σθ (kg/m³): warm light water to the south (red), cold dense water to the north (green); the black wedge is the continental slope. (b) Geostrophic velocity profile v(z) (cm/s); the dashed line marks the reference level.

1
Read the density section · 读取密度断面

Warm, light water (σθ ≈ 26.0–26.5) occupies the southern side; cold, dense water (up to 27.88) lies to the north. Isopycnals are deep in the south and rise toward the north. 南侧为暖而低密的水（σθ ≈ 26.0–26.5），北侧为冷而高密的水（可达 27.88）。等密度面在南侧深、向北抬升。

2
Specific volume anomaly δ · 比容异常

δ = α(S, T, p) − α(35, 0, p). The lighter the water, the larger δ and the taller the water column. Below the surface layer, station A has a larger δ than station B. δ = α(S, T, p) − α(35, 0, p)。水越轻，δ 越大，水柱越高。表层以下 A 站的 δ 大于 B 站。

3
Dynamic height D · 动力高度

D(z) = ∫z0 δ dp, integrated upward from the reference level (here 1000 m). Larger δ ⇒ larger D. In this section ΔD = DA − DB ≈ 0.9 dyn·m — the southern station A is higher. 自参考面（此处取 1000 m）向上积分 D(z) = ∫ δ dp。δ 越大 ⇒ D 越大。本例 ΔD = DA − DB ≈ 0.9 动力米，南侧 A 站更高。

4
Geostrophic velocity v · 地转流速

v = (1/f)·(ΔD/L), with 1 dyn·m = 10 m²/s². At 39°N, f ≈ 9.2 × 10⁻⁵ s⁻¹:
v ≈ (1 / 9.2×10⁻⁵) × (9 m²/s² ÷ 1×10⁵ m) ≈ 0.98 m/s ≈ 100 cm/s, decreasing with depth toward the reference level. v = (1/f)·(ΔD/L)，1 动力米 = 10 m²/s²。39°N 处 f ≈ 9.2×10⁻⁵ s⁻¹：v ≈ 0.98 m/s ≈ 100 cm/s，随深度递减至参考面。

核心规律 · Key rules：
① 密度越低 → 比容越大 → 动力高度（海面高度）越高。低密度水体积更大，整层水柱积分后海面相对隆起。 Lower density ⇒ larger specific volume ⇒ higher dynamic height (sea surface).
② 低密度侧 = 高动力高度侧（本图南侧为高值）。The low-density side is the high-dynamic-height side (here, the south).
③ 压强梯度力由高指向低（南→北）；地转流向垂直于断面、沿等动力高度线流动。 PGF runs high→low; geostrophic flow is perpendicular to the section, along the dynamic-height contours.
⚠ 易错提醒：不是沿断面从高到低流动！记忆法：面朝流向，高压在右 → 本图流向向东。 Pitfall: the flow does NOT run downhill across the section. Rule of thumb — face the flow, high pressure on the right ⇒ here the flow is eastward.
④ 等密度线越密 → 密度梯度越大 → 流速越强；上层密度梯度最大 → 表层流速最快（约 100 cm/s），向深层递减。 Tighter isopycnals ⇒ larger density gradient ⇒ stronger flow; the strongest gradient is in the upper layer, so the surface velocity is fastest (~100 cm/s) and decays with depth.

🌀 Navier–Stokes Equations & Their Reductions · NS 方程及其退化

How the full momentum equation reduces to geostrophic, inertial and Ekman flow. · 完整动量方程在何种条件下退化为地转流、惯性流与埃克曼流。

① 完整 NS 动量方程 · The full momentum equation

In a rotating frame, per unit mass · 旋转坐标系下（单位质量）：

∂u/∂t + (u·∇)u + 2Ω × u = −(1/ρ)∇p + g + F

Horizontal components · 水平分量：

∂u/∂t + u·∇u − f v = −(1/ρ)·∂p/∂x + (1/ρ)·∂τx/∂z
∂v/∂t + u·∇v + f u = −(1/ρ)·∂p/∂y + (1/ρ)·∂τy/∂z

五项 · Five terms： ① 局地加速度 ∂u/∂t (local acceleration)　② 平流 / 惯性 (u·∇)u (advection)　③ 科里奥利 2Ω × u (Coriolis)　④ 压强梯度 (1/ρ)∇p (pressure gradient)　⑤ 摩擦 / 风应力 ∂τ/∂z (friction / wind stress)。 Which terms are kept and which are dropped defines each simplified flow below.

罗斯贝数 · Rossby number：Ro = U / (fL) 度量平流项与科里奥利项之比。Ro ≪ 1 ⇒ 平流可忽略 ⇒ 地转 / 埃克曼近似成立；Ro ≫ 1 ⇒ 平流主导 ⇒ 非地转（惯性流）。 Ro = U/(fL) measures advection against Coriolis. Ro ≪ 1 ⇒ advection negligible ⇒ geostrophic / Ekman; Ro ≫ 1 ⇒ advection dominates ⇒ ageostrophic (inertial).

G
Geostrophic flow · 地转流

Conditions · 条件：Ro ≪ 1（大尺度、缓变），定常 ∂/∂t = 0，无摩擦 F = 0，远离边界。 Large scale, steady, frictionless, away from boundaries.

Retained · 保留：科里奥利 + 压强梯度。Coriolis + pressure gradient.

−f vg = −(1/ρ)·∂p/∂x　　+ f ug = −(1/ρ)·∂p/∂y

Result · 结果：ug = −(1/ρf)·∂p/∂y，vg = (1/ρf)·∂p/∂x。沿等压线流动，北半球高压在右（见上方湾流算例）。 Flow along isobars, with high pressure on the right in the NH.

I
Inertial flow · 惯性流

Conditions · 条件：无压强梯度 ∇p ≈ 0，无摩擦，不受边界影响（孤立水质点）。 No pressure gradient, frictionless, isolated from boundaries.

Retained · 保留：局地加速度 + 科里奥利。Local acceleration + Coriolis.

∂u/∂t = f v　　∂v/∂t = −f u

Result · 结果：等速圆周运动（惯性振荡），周期 T = 2π/f，半径 R = V/f；北半球顺时针旋转。 Uniform circular motion (inertial oscillation): T = 2π/f, R = V/f; clockwise in the NH.

E
Ekman flow · 埃克曼流

Conditions · 条件：定常，f 平面，层内压强梯度可忽略，但海面存在风应力。 Steady, f-plane, PGF negligible inside the layer, but wind stress at the surface.

Retained · 保留：科里奥利 + 垂向应力散度。Coriolis + vertical stress divergence.

−f v = (1/ρ)·∂τx/∂z　　+ f u = (1/ρ)·∂τy/∂z

Result · 结果：埃克曼螺线；北半球表层流偏于风向右侧 45°，净输运偏右 90°：M = τ/(ρf)；抽吸 wE = curl(τ)/(ρf)。 Ekman spiral; NH surface current 45° right of the wind, net transport 90° right: M = τ/(ρf); pumping wE = curl(τ)/(ρf).

流型 Flow 保留项 Retained 忽略项 Neglected 关键条件 Key condition
地转流 Geostrophic 科里奥利 + 压强梯度 加速度、摩擦 Ro ≪ 1，定常，无摩擦
惯性流 Inertial 局地加速度 + 科里奥利 压强梯度、摩擦 ∇p ≈ 0
埃克曼流 Ekman 科里奥利 + 应力散度 压强梯度、加速度 定常，f 平面，风应力
