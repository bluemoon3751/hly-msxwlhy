15 bilingual Q&A on heat budget, mixed layer, thermocline, dynamics and more. · 15 道双语问答，涵盖热收支、混合层、温跃层、动力学等主题。

1
What factors determine the heat budget terms Qs, Qb, Qe and Qh, respectively? Make clear the importance of each factor of each term.
决定热收支项 Qs、Qb、Qe、Qh 的因子分别是什么？请说明每一项各因子的重要性。
EN

Qs · Shortwave solar radiation Incoming solar energy absorbed by the sea surface. Key factors:

  • Solar angle / latitude & season — the dominant control on incoming flux.
  • Cloud cover — reflects/scatters a large fraction back to space.
  • Atmospheric scattering & absorption (ozone, water vapor, aerosols).
  • Surface albedo — open ocean reflects little (~6%), ice reflects much.

Qb · Longwave back radiation Net infrared loss from the sea surface. Key factors:

  • Sea-surface temperature (SST) — governs emission via Stefan–Boltzmann law.
  • Cloud cover & water vapor — warm, moist atmosphere returns IR (greenhouse).
  • Air temperature — sets the downward counter-radiation.

Qe · Latent heat flux (evaporation) Heat carried away by vaporization. Key factors:

  • Wind speed — removes vapor, sets the exchange coefficient.
  • Sea–air humidity difference — vapor pressure gradient.
  • SST — higher SST raises saturation vapor pressure.

Qh · Sensible heat flux Direct conductive/convective heat transfer. Key factors:

  • Sea–air temperature difference — the driving gradient.
  • Wind speed — turbulent exchange coefficient.
中文

Qs · 短波太阳辐射 海面吸收的入射太阳能量。关键因子：

  • 太阳高度角 / 纬度与季节 —— 入射通量的主控因子。
  • 云量 —— 将大量辐射反射/散射回太空。
  • 大气散射与吸收（臭氧、水汽、气溶胶）。
  • 表面反照率 —— 开阔大洋反照率低（约 6%），海冰反照率高。

Qb · 长波回辐射 海面向外的净红外辐射损失。关键因子：

  • 海表温度（SST） —— 通过 Stefan–Boltzmann 定律决定发射强度。
  • 云量与水汽 —— 暖湿大气会把红外辐射回送（温室效应）。
  • 气温 —— 决定向下的反向辐射。

Qe · 潜热通量（蒸发） 随汽化带走的热量。关键因子：

  • 风速 —— 移走水汽并设定交换系数。
  • 海气湿度差 —— 水汽压梯度。
  • SST —— SST 越高，饱和水汽压越大。

Qh · 感热通量 直接的传导/对流热交换。关键因子：

  • 海气温差 —— 驱动梯度。
  • 风速 —— 湍流交换系数。

What are the typical vertical temperature and salinity profiles in the subtropical and subpolar regions of the North Pacific?
北太平洋副热带与副极地区域的典型温度、盐度垂直剖面是怎样的？
EN

Subtropical North Pacific

  • Temperature: warm, well-stratified surface layer (~50–100 m) over a strong permanent thermocline (~200–1000 m) where T drops rapidly.
  • Salinity: high surface salinity (evaporation exceeds precipitation), with a subsurface salinity maximum near the top of the thermocline.

Subpolar North Pacific

  • Temperature: cold throughout the upper ocean; weak or absent permanent thermocline — temperature may even increase with depth (temperature inversion beneath the halocline).
  • Salinity: low surface salinity (excess precipitation), a strong halocline in the upper few hundred meters.
中文

副热带北太平洋

  • 温度：表层暖且层结稳定（约 50–100 m），其下为强永久温跃层（约 200–1000 m），温度迅速下降。
  • 盐度：表层盐度高（蒸发大于降水），在温跃层顶部附近出现次表层盐度极大值。

副极地北太平洋

  • 温度：上层整层偏冷；永久温跃层弱甚至不存在 —— 温度甚至可能随深度升高（盐跃层下方的温度倒置）。
  • 盐度：表层盐度低（降水过剩），上部数百米存在强盐跃层。

What are the dominant regions of net evaporation in the ocean?
海洋中净蒸发占主导的区域有哪些？
EN
  • Subtropical gyres (≈ 15–35° latitude) in both hemispheres, especially the trade-wind belts.
  • Mediterranean Sea — strong E–P excess, a net evaporative basin.
  • Red Sea — one of the highest evaporation rates on Earth.
  • Arabian Sea & subtropical North Atlantic — also large net evaporation centers.

These regions are the source of the intermediate/deep water-mass formation that drives the shallow overturning cell.

  • 地中海 —— E–P 失衡强烈，属净蒸发盆地。
  • 红海 —— 全球蒸发率最高的海域之一。
  • 阿拉伯海与副热带北大西洋 —— 同为净蒸发中心。

这些区域是驱动浅层翻转环流的中间 / 深水团形成源地。

What are the key components of the Mixed Layer heat budget?
混合层热收支的关键组成部分有哪些？
EN

The mixed-layer heat budget balances all fluxes that change the upper-ocean heat content:

∂(ρ·cp·h·T)/∂t = Qs − Qb − Qe − Qh − Qpen + Qadv + Qent

  • Qs — shortwave solar radiation (penetrates to the diurnal layer).
  • Qb — longwave back radiation.
  • Qe — latent heat flux (evaporation).
  • Qh — sensible heat flux.
  • Qpen — shortwave that penetrates below the mixed layer.
  • Qadv — horizontal advective heat convergence.
  • Qent — entrainment/mixing at the base of the mixed layer.
中文

混合层热收支平衡所有改变上层海洋热含量的通量：

∂(ρ·cp·h·T)/∂t = Qs − Qb − Qe − Qh − Qpen + Qadv + Qent

  • Qs —— 短波太阳辐射（穿透至日变层）。
  • Qb —— 长波回辐射。
  • Qe —— 潜热通量（蒸发）。
  • Qh —— 感热通量。
  • Qpen —— 穿透混合层底部的短波辐射。
  • Qadv —— 水平平流热辐合。
  • Qent —— 混合层底部的卷入 / 混合。

Is the North Atlantic Ocean saltier or fresher on average than the Pacific? Why?
北大西洋平均盐度比太平洋高还是低？为什么？
EN

The North Atlantic is saltier than the Pacific on average.

  • Net evaporation exceeds precipitation in the Atlantic, while the Pacific receives more rain (ITCZ, monsoons).
  • The Atlantic imports high-salinity water from the Mediterranean outflow.
  • Atmospheric vapor transport carries fresh water from the Atlantic basin to the Pacific across Central America.
  • Result: Atlantic surface salinity ~35–36 psu vs. Pacific ~34–34.5 psu — a key driver of the Atlantic Meridional Overturning Circulation (AMOC).
中文

北大西洋平均更咸。

  • 大西洋净蒸发大于降水，而太平洋降水更丰沛（ITCZ、季风）。
  • 大西洋还接受地中海外流水带来的高盐水。
  • 大气水汽输送把淡水从中美洲跨流域带往太平洋。
  • 结果：大西洋表层盐度约 35–36 psu，太平洋约 34–34.5 psu —— 这是大西洋经向翻转环流（AMOC）的关键驱动因素。

What regions of the ocean are characterized by large differences from summer to winter? What regions have the least seasonal variability?
海洋中哪些区域夏冬差异显著？哪些区域季节变率最小？
EN

Large seasonal contrast

  • Mid-latitude western boundary current regions — Kuroshio, Gulf Stream (warm in summer, fierce heat loss in winter).
  • Monsoonal seas — especially the northern Indian Ocean / Arabian Sea.
  • Subtropical gyres — strong summer stratification vs. winter mixing.

Least seasonal variability

  • Equatorial & tropical oceans — small annual solar-angle variation.
  • Deep ocean below the seasonal thermocline — insulated from the surface cycle.
  • Polar regions are seasonally variable at the surface but the deep basins are steady.

夏冬差异显著

  • 中纬度西边界流区 —— 黑潮、湾流（夏季暖，冬季强烈失热）。
  • 季风海区 —— 尤其是北印度洋 / 阿拉伯海。
  • 副热带环流 —— 夏季强层结 vs. 冬季强混合。

季节变率最小

  • 赤道与热带海洋 —— 全年太阳高度角变化小。
  • 季节温跃层以下的深海 —— 与表层循环隔离。

Please describe the two processes that maintain the thermocline.
请描述维持温跃层的两个过程。
EN
  • 1. Mechanical / vertical mixing — wind stress and shear-driven turbulence at the base of the mixed layer mix warm surface water downward, sharpening and maintaining the upper thermocline gradient.
  • 2. Subduction & advection along density surfaces — water masses formed at the surface in subtropical/subpolar regions slide (subduct) along isopycnals into the thermocline interior, ventilating it and setting the layered T–S structure of the permanent thermocline.

Together: mixing sharpens the top, while subduction ventilates the interior.

中文
  • 1. 机械 / 垂向混合 —— 风应力与混合层底的剪切湍流把暖的表层水向下混合，使上层温跃层梯度更锐利并得以维持。
  • 2. 沿密度面的俯冲与平流 —— 副热带 / 副极地表层形成的水团沿等密度面（俯冲）进入温跃层内部，对其进行通风并塑造永久温跃层的层状 T–S 结构。

两者协同：混合锐化顶部，俯冲通风内部。

Please summarize the four families of statistical / analytical methods commonly used in physical-oceanography data analysis, and explain each.
请归纳物理海洋数据分析中常用的四大类统计 / 分析方法，并逐一讲解。
EN

Source slide lists four methods: Basic concepts, Time series analysis, Multi-dimensional data analysis, and Empirical Orthogonal Functions (EOFs). Each is summarized below.

① Basic concepts · 基础概念

  • Random vs. systematic error — random error is zero-mean, unpredictable noise; systematic error is a consistent bias that must be calibrated out.
  • Mean, variance, standard deviation — describe central tendency and spread of a sample.
  • Correlation & covariance — quantify the linear relationship (signed strength / shared variance) between two variables.

② Time series analysis · 时间序列分析

  • Also known as spectral / Fourier / harmonic analysis.
  • Can be performed in the time (frequency) domain or the spatial (wavenumber) domain.
  • Identifies dominant frequencies of variability — especially useful when the forcing has a well-defined frequency (e.g. tides, annual cycle).
  • The shape of the spectrum reveals underlying physics: a red spectrum (more power at low frequencies) implies persistence / memory typical of geophysical systems; a white spectrum implies pure randomness.

③ Multi-dimensional data analysis · 多维数据分析（客观分析）

  • Objective analysis (a.k.a. optimal interpolation / Kriging) maps spatially non-uniform observations onto a regular grid.
  • It incorporates the correlation length scale and the noise of observations.
  • The grid estimate is a weighted sum of nearby observations, with weights chosen to minimize mean-square error.

④ Empirical Orthogonal Functions (EOFs) · 经验正交函数

  • Also known as Principal Component Analysis (PCA) or Factor Analysis.
  • Provides a compact description of the principal spatial and temporal variability of a dataset.
  • Called empirical because the spatial structures are defined by the data itself, as opposed to a set of pre-prescribed mathematical basis functions (e.g. sine waves, Bessel functions, Legendre polynomials).
  • Output: a small set of orthogonal spatial modes (EOFs) each paired with a time-varying amplitude (Principal Component, PC), ordered by the fraction of variance explained.
中文

课件将方法归纳为四类：基础概念、时间序列分析、多维数据分析、经验正交函数 EOF。逐条如下。

① 基础概念 · Basic concepts

  • 随机误差与系统误差 —— 随机误差为零均值、不可预测的噪声；系统误差是恒定偏倚，需要标定去除。
  • 均值、方差、标准差 —— 描述样本的中心趋势与离散程度。
  • 相关与协方差 —— 量化两个变量之间的线性关系（带符号强度 / 共享方差）。

② 时间序列分析 · Time series analysis

  • 又称谱分析 / 傅里叶分析 / 谐波分析。
  • 可在时间（频率）域或空间（波数）域中进行。
  • 提取变率的主频率 —— 当外强迫频率明确（如潮汐、年循环）时尤为有用。
  • 谱形能反映物理机制：红谱（低频能量更强）暗示地球物理系统典型的持续性 / 记忆性；白谱则意味着纯随机。

③ 多维数据分析（客观分析）· Multi-dimensional data analysis

  • 客观分析（又称最优插值 / 克里金）将空间分布不均匀的观测映射到规则网格上。
  • 纳入相关长度尺度与观测噪声。
  • 网格估计值是邻近观测的加权和，权重按最小均方误差准则确定。

④ 经验正交函数 EOF · Empirical Orthogonal Functions

  • 又称主成分分析（PCA）或因子分析。
  • 对数据集的主要时空变率给出紧凑描述。
  • 之所以称为经验，是因为空间结构由数据本身定义，而非预先设定的数学基函数（如正弦波、贝塞尔函数、勒让德多项式）。
  • 输出：少量相互正交的空间模态（EOF），每个配一条随时间变化的振幅（主成分 PC），按解释方差大小排序。

Work out the period of inertial oscillations at the north pole, 30°N, equator, and south pole.
计算北极、北纬 30°、赤道和南极处惯性振荡的周期。
EN

Period of inertial oscillations: T = 2π / f, where f = 2Ω·sinφ is the Coriolis parameter.
Earth's angular velocity Ω ≈ 7.292 × 10⁻⁵ rad/s

  • North Pole (90°N): f = 2Ω ≈ 1.458×10⁻⁴ s⁻¹, period T ≈ 12 hours (half a sidereal day, counterclockwise)
  • 30°N: f = 2Ω·sin30° = Ω ≈ 7.292×10⁻⁵ s⁻¹, period T ≈ 24 hours (one sidereal day)
  • Equator (0°): f = 0, period T → infinity (no inertial oscillations at the equator)
  • South Pole (90°S): f = −2Ω, |f| = 2Ω, period T ≈ 12 hours (clockwise — opposite sense to NH)
中文

惯性振荡周期公式：T = 2π / f，其中 f = 2Ω·sinφ 为科里奥利参数。
地球自转角速度 Ω ≈ 7.292 × 10⁻⁵ rad/s

  • 北极（90°N）：f = 2Ω ≈ 1.458×10⁻⁴ s⁻¹，周期 T ≈ 12 小时（约半个恒星日，逆时针旋转）
  • 北纬 30°：f = 2Ω·sin30° = Ω ≈ 7.292×10⁻⁵ s⁻¹，周期 T ≈ 24 小时（约一个恒星日）
  • 赤道（0°）：f = 0，周期 T → 无穷大（赤道处无惯性振荡）
  • 南极（90°S）：f = −2Ω，|f| = 2Ω，周期 T ≈ 12 小时（顺时针旋转，与北半球方向相反）

In geostrophic flow, what direction is the Coriolis force in relation to the pressure gradient force? What direction is it in relation to the velocity?
在地转流中，科里奥利力相对于压强梯度力的方向是什么？相对于速度的方向呢？
EN
  • Relative to the pressure gradient force: equal in magnitude and opposite in direction — the two forces exactly balance (geostrophic balance).
  • Relative to the velocity: perpendicular to the flow direction — to the right in the Northern Hemisphere, to the left in the Southern Hemisphere.
  • Consequence: geostrophic flow runs parallel to isobars, with high pressure on the right (NH) / left (SH) of the current.
中文
  • 相对于压强梯度力：大小相等、方向相反——两者恰好平衡（地转平衡）。
  • 相对于速度方向：垂直于速度方向——北半球指向速度的右侧，南半球指向速度的左侧。
  • 结果：地转流平行于等压线流动，高压在流速的右侧（北半球）/ 左侧（南半球），即"顺流而立，高压在右"。

What is a "level of no motion"? Why would a "level of known motion" be a better choice for the same kind of calculation? (What can we actually compute about the velocity structure given the density distribution and an assumption of geostrophy?)
什么是"无运动面"？为什么"已知运动面"是同类计算中更好的选择？（给定密度分布和地转平衡假设，我们实际上能计算出速度结构的什么信息？）
EN
  • Level of no motion: an assumed reference depth (e.g., 2000 m or the seafloor) where we set horizontal velocity to zero. We then compute geostrophic shear from the density field via the thermal wind relation and integrate upward to obtain absolute velocity.
  • Problem: a truly motionless level almost never exists — deep currents persist (deep western boundary currents, abyssal circulation).
  • Level of known motion: uses actually measured velocities (from LADCP, Argo floats, moorings) at some depth as the reference, removing the error of the "no motion" assumption — hence the better choice.
  • What density alone gives us: only the vertical shear of geostrophic velocity (the thermal wind), i.e. the relative velocity structure. Absolute speed requires a reference level — this is the fundamental indeterminacy of the geostrophic calculation.
中文
  • 无运动面（level of no motion）：假设某一参考深度（例如 2000 m 或海底）的水平流速为零，然后利用热成风关系从密度场计算地转切变，向上积分得到绝对流速。
  • 问题：真正流速为零的深度几乎不存在——深层海流（如深层西边界流、深渊环流）始终存在。
  • 已知运动面（level of known motion）：用实际观测数据（如 LADCP、Argo 浮标、潜标）在某一深度测得的真实流速作为参考，避免了"无运动"假设带来的误差，因此更优。
  • 仅靠密度能算出什么：只能得到地转速度的垂直切变（即热成风），也就是速度的相对结构。绝对速度必须依赖参考面——这是地转计算的根本不确定性。

Name the three contributing factors to potential vorticity.
列出位势涡度（位涡）的三个贡献因素。
EN

Potential vorticity (PV) combines three ingredients:

  1. Planetary vorticity (f) — vorticity from Earth's rotation, i.e. the Coriolis parameter, which varies with latitude (f = 2Ω·sinφ).
  2. Relative vorticity (ζ) — the spin of a fluid parcel relative to the Earth, arising from horizontal shear and curvature of the flow.
  3. Layer thickness / stratification (h or ∂ρ/∂z) — the vertical extent of the water column or the vertical density gradient, which captures the stretching or squashing of vortex tubes.

Simple form for a homogeneous layer: PV = (f + ζ) / h
Ertel form in stratified fluids (on isopycnals): PV = (f + ζθ) · ∂ρ/∂z

中文

位势涡度（Potential Vorticity, PV）综合了以下三个要素：

  1. 行星涡度（f）——由地球自转产生，即科里奥利参数，随纬度变化（f = 2Ω·sinφ）。
  2. 相对涡度（ζ）——流体微团自身的旋转（相对于地球的涡度），由流速的水平切变和曲率决定。

How does Ekman pumping connect to the Sverdrup interior flow? Explain using potential vorticity arguments.
埃克曼抽吸如何与斯维尔德鲁普内区流联系起来？用位涡的观点解释。
EN
  • Ekman pumping (wE): vertical velocity at the base of the wind-driven Ekman layer, wE = curl(τ) / (ρf). In the NH subtropical gyre, the (negative) clockwise wind curl drives downward Ekman pumping.
  • Sverdrup balance: the interior geostrophic transport satisfies βV = curl(τ)/ρ — the vertically integrated meridional transport equals the wind stress curl divided by ρβ.

PV argument:

  • In the ocean interior, potential vorticity is approximately conserved: D(f/h)/Dt ≈ 0 (neglecting relative vorticity and mixing).
  • In the subtropical gyre, downward Ekman pumping pushes surface water into the interior — the water column is squashed (h decreases).
  • To conserve PV (f/h ≈ constant), f must decrease as h decreases.
  • f decreases toward the equator, so parcels move equatorward — this is the Sverdrup interior flow.
  • The gyre closes via a narrow, fast western boundary current (Kuroshio, Gulf Stream) that returns water poleward.
中文
  • 埃克曼抽吸（wE）：风生埃克曼输运的辐合/辐散在埃克曼层底产生的垂直速度，wE = curl(τ) / (ρf)。在北半球副热带环流中，风应力旋度为负（顺时针），产生向下的埃克曼抽吸。

  • 大洋内区位涡近似守恒：D(f/h)/Dt ≈ 0（忽略相对涡度和混合）。
  • 在副热带环流中，向下的埃克曼抽吸把表层水压入内区——水柱被压扁（h 减小）。
  • 为保持位涡 f/h 近似不变，f 必须随 h 减小而减小。
  • f 向赤道方向减小，因此水团向赤道方向流动——这就是斯维尔德鲁普内区流。
  • 环流通过狭窄而快速的西边界流（如黑潮、湾流）向极地方向回流，从而闭合整个副热带环流圈。

Under what conditions can the Coriolis force be neglected for gravity waves?
在什么条件下重力波可以不考虑科氏力的作用？
EN

The Coriolis effect can be neglected when the wave's period is much shorter than the local inertial period (i.e., Twave << 2π/f), or equivalently, when the wave frequency ω is much larger than f (ω >> f).

  • Short-period surface gravity waves (wind waves, swell): typical periods of seconds to ~20 s — far shorter than inertial periods of hours to days.
  • Small spatial scale relative to the Rossby radius of deformation.
  • Rule of thumb: if the wave period < 1/10 of the local inertial period, the Coriolis deflection during one wave cycle is negligible.

Conversely, for long waves (tsunamis, tides, Rossby waves, Kelvin waves) with periods of hours to days/years, the Coriolis force becomes important and cannot be ignored.

中文

当波的周期远小于当地惯性周期（T波 << 2π/f），或者说波频率 ω 远大于 f（ω >> f）时，科氏力可以忽略。

  • 短周期表面重力波（风浪、涌浪）：周期几秒到约 20 秒，远小于数小时到数天的惯性周期。
  • 空间尺度远小于罗斯贝变形半径。

反之，对于长波（海啸、潮汐、罗斯贝波、开尔文波等），周期为数小时到数天/年，科氏力变得重要，不能忽略。

Are gravity waves, inertia–gravity waves, and Rossby waves dispersive? Why?
重力波、惯性重力波和罗斯贝波是否弥散？为什么？
EN

① Surface gravity waves — dispersive

  • Dispersion relation (deep water): ω² = gk
  • Phase speed c = ω/k = √(g/k), which depends on k — different wavelengths travel at different speeds.
  • Group speed cg = dω/dk = c/2 — energy propagates at half the phase speed.

② Inertia–gravity waves — dispersive

  • Dispersion relation: ω² = f² + N²k²/m² (or ω² = f² + gHk² for shallow water with rotation)
  • Phase speed depends on wavenumber and the angle of propagation relative to the vertical — clearly dispersive.

③ Rossby waves — dispersive

  • Dispersion relation: ω = −βk / (k² + l² + f₀²/(gH))
  • Phase speed c = ω/k = −β / (k² + l² + f₀²/(gH)), which varies with wavenumber.
  • Longer waves propagate faster (westward), shorter waves slower — classic dispersion.

Exception: shallow-water gravity waves (ω = k√(gh)) are non-dispersive — all wavelengths travel at the same speed c = √(gh).

中文

① 表面重力波 — 频散（弥散）

  • 频散关系（深水）：ω² = gk
  • 相速度 c = ω/k = √(g/k)，依赖于波数 k——不同波长以不同速度传播。
  • 群速度 cg = dω/dk = c/2 —— 能量以相速度的一半传播。

② 惯性重力波 — 频散（弥散）

  • 频散关系：ω² = f² + N²k²/m²（或浅水旋转情况下 ω² = f² + gHk²）
  • 相速度随波数和传播方向（与垂直夹角）变化——明显频散。

③ 罗斯贝波 — 频散（弥散）

  • 频散关系：ω = −βk / (k² + l² + f₀²/(gH))
  • 相速度 c = ω/k = −β / (k² + l² + f₀²/(gH))，随波数变化。
  • 长波传播（西传）更快，短波更慢——典型频散。

例外：浅水波重力波（ω = k√(gh)）是非频散的——所有波长以相同速度 c = √(gh) 传播。

What causes equatorial waveguides, and which wave types can exist there?
赤道波系形成波导的原因是什么？可以产生哪几种波动？
EN

Cause of the equatorial waveguide:

  • The Coriolis parameter f = 2Ω·sinφ is zero at the equator and increases with latitude on both sides.
  • This creates a trapping mechanism: waves that try to propagate poleward encounter increasing f, which bends their energy back toward the equator (analogous to total internal reflection).
  • The effective waveguide width is set by the equatorial Rossby radius of deformation, Req = √(gH) / β (typically ~200–300 km for the ocean thermocline).

Wave types in the equatorial waveguide:

  1. Equatorial Kelvin waves — eastward-propagating, trapped to the equator, non-dispersive, symmetric about the equator.
  2. Equatorial Rossby waves — westward-propagating, trapped, dispersive, symmetric.
  3. Mixed Rossby–gravity waves (Yanai waves) — eastward group velocity, unusual dispersion curve.
  4. Equatorial inertia–gravity waves (Poincaré waves) — east and west propagating, higher frequency.
中文

赤道波导形成原因：

  • 科里奥利参数 f = 2Ω·sinφ 在赤道处为零，向两侧随纬度增大。

What are the types of continental-shelf long waves, and what are their propagation characteristics?
陆架长波有哪几种，其传播有什么特点？
EN

Continental-shelf long waves are low-frequency, long-wavelength motions trapped along the coast by the combination of bathymetry and the Coriolis effect. The main types:

  1. Continental shelf waves (barotropic)
    • Restoring mechanism: topographic β-effect — the change of f/h with cross-shelf distance.
    • Propagation: with the coast on the right in the Northern Hemisphere (poleward along eastern boundaries, equatorward along western boundaries).
    • Non-dispersive or weakly dispersive; periods of days to weeks; energy trapped on the shelf.
  2. Coastal Kelvin waves
    • Restoring mechanism: gravity + Coriolis, trapped against a vertical wall coast.
    • Propagation: with the coast on the right in the NH; phase speed c = √(gH), non-dispersive alongshore.
    • Amplitude decays exponentially offshore (e-folding scale = Rossby radius).
  3. Coastally trapped waves (general category)
    • Includes shelf waves, coastal Kelvin waves, and their continuous modes.
    • All share: energy propagates with the coast on the right (NH); amplitude maximum nearshore.

What are the basic conditions for internal waves to occur in the ocean?
海洋内波发生的基本条件是什么？
EN

Internal waves occur within the density-stratified interior of the ocean. The basic conditions are:

  1. Stable stratification — density increases with depth (dρ/dz < 0 in the z-up convention). This provides the buoyancy restoring force.
    • Quantified by the Brunt–Väisälä frequency: N = √(−(g/ρ)·dρ/dz).
    • N must be real and positive — the fluid must be statically stable.
  2. A disturbance / forcing that displaces fluid parcels from their equilibrium level. Common sources:
    • Flow over bottom topography (tide over a ridge, current over a sill).
    • Wind stress and inertial oscillations in the mixed layer base.
    • Interaction of surface waves with the thermocline.
    • Geostrophic adjustment and mesoscale eddy processes.
  3. Frequency condition — the wave frequency ω must satisfy f < ω < N (for inertia–gravity internal waves).
    • If ω > N: stratification cannot provide enough restoring force; no wave propagation.
    • If ω < f: rotation dominates; the motion becomes quasi-geostrophic rather than wave-like.

Why the pycnocline / thermocline matters: the strongest density gradients (largest N) occur at the pycnocline, so internal waves are most intense there and often propagate along it.

中文

内波发生在密度层化的海洋内部。基本条件：

  1. 稳定层结——密度随深度增大（z 向上时 dρ/dz < 0），提供浮力恢复力。
    • 用布伦特-维萨拉频率 N = √(−(g/ρ)·dρ/dz) 量化。
    • N 必须为正实数——流体必须静力稳定。
  2. 扰动 / 强迫源，将流体微团推离平衡位置。常见来源：
    • 底地形上的流动（潮汐过海脊、流过海槛）。
    • 风应力与混合层底的惯性振荡。
    • 表面波与温跃层的相互作用。
    • 地转调整与中尺度涡过程。
  3. 频率条件——波频率 ω 满足 f < ω < N（惯性重力内波）。
    • 若 ω > N：层结提供的恢复力不足，无法传播。
    • 若 ω < f：旋转占主导，运动呈准地转而非波动形式。

为何跃层重要：密度梯度最强（N 最大）的地方在密度跃层/温跃层，因此内波在此最强，且常沿跃层传播。

What is the generation mechanism of tsunamis? Is their long-distance propagation affected by the Coriolis force? Why?
海啸的发生机制是什么？长距离传输是否受科氏力的影响？为什么？
EN

Generation mechanism:

  • Tsunamis are generated by sudden, large-scale vertical displacement of the seafloor, which displaces the overlying water column and sets up long surface gravity waves.
  • Common triggers:
    • Submarine earthquakes (fault slip) — the dominant cause (~80% of tsunamis).
    • Submarine landslides (volcanic flank collapse, sediment slides).
    • Volcanic eruptions (caldera collapse, pyroclastic flows into water).
    • Meteorite impacts (very rare).

Coriolis effect on long-distance propagation — YES, but modest:

  • Tsunamis are shallow-water waves (wavelength ~100–500 km >> ocean depth ~4 km), so c = √(gH) ≈ 700–900 km/h.
  • Their periods are 10 min to ~1 hour — much shorter than the inertial period at mid-latitudes (~12–24 h), so the Coriolis effect is weak over one wave cycle.
  • However, over ocean-basin scales (hours to days), the cumulative effect of the Coriolis force is not entirely negligible: it can gradually deflect the propagation direction and influence the structure of the wave field (e.g., by generating Coriolis-modified waves or by affecting how tsunamis refract around ocean basins).
  • Bottom line: for first-order tsunami travel-time forecasting, the Coriolis effect is usually ignored (shallow-water non-rotating theory is sufficient). For detailed basin-scale modeling and coastal amplification, rotation may be included.
中文

发生机制：

  • 海啸由海底突然的、大尺度的垂直位移产生，使上覆水柱位移并激发长表面重力波。
  • 常见触发因素：
    • 海底地震（断层滑动）——最主要的原因（约 80% 的海啸）。
    • 海底滑坡（火山侧翼坍塌、沉积物滑塌）。
    • 火山喷发（破火山口坍塌、火山碎屑流入水）。
    • 陨石撞击（极罕见）。

  • 海啸是浅水波（波长 ~100–500 km >> 海深 ~4 km），所以 c = √(gH) ≈ 700–900 km/h。
  • 其周期为 10 分钟到约 1 小时——远短于中纬度惯性周期（~12–24 小时），因此在一个波周期内科氏效应很弱。
  • 然而，在大洋尺度（数小时到数天）的传播中，科氏力的累积效应并非完全可以忽略：它可以逐渐偏转传播方向，并影响波场结构（例如产生科氏修正波或影响海啸在洋盆中的折射方式）。

What is the difference between phase speed and group speed? Why is group speed important?

EN

Phase speed (c = ω/k) is the speed at which individual wave crests travel. Group speed (cg = dω/dk) is the speed at which wave energy propagates, and also the speed at which wave groups / envelopes move.

Group speed is important because energy (and information) travels at the group speed, not the phase speed. For dispersive waves like deep-water gravity waves, cg = c/2 — energy moves at half the speed of the crests.

中文

相速度（c = ω/k）是单个波峰传播的速度。群速度（cg = dω/dk）是波能量传播的速度，也是波群/波包移动的速度。

群速度重要是因为能量（和信息）以群速度传播，而非相速度。对于深水重力波等频散波，cg = c/2——能量以波峰速度的一半传播。

📖 Definition · 定义
Dynamical Oceanography — to describe and understand oceanic processes via simple laws such as Newton's laws of motion. 动力海洋学——通过牛顿运动定律等简洁规律来描述和理解海洋过程。

(2) Ocean is a continuum instead of discrete particles — we deal with fields (velocity, density, pressure) distributed continuously in space and time, requiring calculus of continua. （2）海洋是连续介质而非离散粒子——我们研究的是在时空中连续分布的场（速度、密度、压强），需要连续介质微积分。

🔢 Nine Core Concepts (in progressive order) · 九大核心概念

Each concept builds on the previous one, gradually adding layers of physical reality · 每个概念以前一个为基础，逐步叠加物理现实的层次

1
Geostrophy · 地转平衡

The simplest dynamical balance: Coriolis force exactly balances the horizontal pressure gradient. Flow is along isobars, with high pressure on the right (NH). This is the foundation of nearly all large-scale ocean circulation theory. 最简单的动力平衡：科里奥利力与水平压强梯度力精确平衡。流动沿等压线，北半球高压在右。这是几乎所有大尺度海洋环流理论的基础。

2
Geostrophy & Friction → Ekman Layer & Upwelling Dynamics · 地转 + 摩擦 → 埃克曼层与上升流动力学

Adding friction (wind stress at the sea surface) breaks pure geostrophy and creates the Ekman spiral: surface current 45° to the right of the wind, net transport 90° to the right. Ekman divergence/pumping drives coastal upwelling and drives the interior circulation. 加入摩擦（海面风应力）打破了纯地转平衡，产生埃克曼螺线：表层流偏风向右侧45°，净输运偏右90°。埃克曼辐散/抽吸驱动沿岸上升流并驱动内区环流。

3
Geostrophy & Stratification → Gulf Stream Structure & River Plumes · 地转 + 层结 → 湾流结构与河流羽流

Adding vertical stratification (density variation with depth) links the horizontal density field to the vertical shear of geostrophic velocity via the thermal wind relation. This explains why western boundary currents like the Gulf Stream are concentrated in the upper ocean, and how buoyant river plumes spread and turn. 加入垂直层结（密度随深度变化），通过热成风关系将水平密度场与地转流速的垂直切变联系起来。这解释了为何湾流等西边界流集中在上层海洋，以及浮力河流羽流如何扩散与转向。

4
Vorticity · 涡度

Vorticity is the local spin of a fluid parcel (twice the angular velocity). It comes in two forms: planetary vorticity (from Earth's rotation, i.e. the Coriolis parameter f) and relative vorticity (ζ, from curvature and shear of the flow itself). Vorticity is the currency of rotating-fluid dynamics. 涡度是流体微团的局部旋转（角速度的两倍）。分为两种：行星涡度（来自地球自转，即科里奥利参数 f）和相对涡度（ζ，来自流动本身的曲率与切变）。涡度是旋转流体动力学的"流通货币"。

5
Potential Vorticity · 位势涡度（位涡）

Potential vorticity (PV) combines vorticity with the thickness (or stratification) of a fluid column. In its simplest form: PV = (f + ζ) / h. PV is a powerful diagnostic because it is approximately conserved following a fluid parcel in adiabatic, frictionless flow. 位势涡度（PV）将涡度与流体柱厚度（或层结）结合起来。最简形式：PV = (f + ζ) / h。位涡是一个强大的诊断量，因为在绝热、无摩擦流动中，它随流体微团近似守恒。

6
Conservation of PV + Coastally Trapped Waves (Rossby, Continental Shelf & Kelvin Waves) · 位涡守恒 + 沿岸捕获波（罗斯贝波、陆架波与开尔文波）

PV conservation gives rise to wave motions that carry information across the ocean. Rossby waves propagate westward and are fundamental to ocean adjustment. Kelvin waves and continental shelf waves are trapped along coasts and carry signals poleward/equatorward along boundaries. 位涡守恒催生了在海洋中传递信息的波动运动。罗斯贝波向西传播，是海洋调整的基础。开尔文波和陆架波被捕获在沿岸，沿边界向极地/向赤道传递信号。

7
Sverdrup Relationship + Davidson Current off California · 斯维尔德鲁普关系 + 加利福尼亚戴维森流

The Sverdrup balance connects wind-stress curl to the meridional transport of the interior ocean: βV = curl(τ)/ρ. Along the California coast, the southward California Current and the poleward undercurrent (Davidson Current) are direct consequences of this balance and the coastal geometry. 斯维尔德鲁普平衡将风应力旋度与大洋内区经向输运联系起来：βV = curl(τ)/ρ。在加利福尼亚沿岸，南向的加利福尼亚流与极向的潜流（戴维森流）正是这一平衡与海岸几何共同作用的直接结果。

8
Western Boundary Currents → Gulf Stream and the Kuroshio · 西边界流 → 湾流与黑潮

Sverdrup theory explains the interior of the gyres but not how the circulation closes. The answer is narrow, fast western boundary currents — the Gulf Stream in the Atlantic and the Kuroshio in the Pacific — where nonlinear inertia and bottom friction become important. These are the "jets" of the ocean. 斯维尔德鲁普理论解释了环流内区，但无法解释环流如何闭合。答案是狭窄而快速的西边界流——大西洋的湾流和太平洋的黑潮——在这些区域非线性惯性和底摩擦变得重要。它们是海洋中的"喷气流"。

9
El Niño and Southern Oscillation (ENSO) · 厄尔尼诺与南方涛动

The culmination of dynamical oceanography coupled with atmospheric dynamics: ENSO is the dominant mode of interannual climate variability on Earth. It involves ocean–atmosphere interaction in the tropical Pacific, with Kelvin/Rossby waves, equatorial upwelling, and the Bjerknes feedback all playing key roles. 动力海洋学与大气动力学耦合的巅峰：ENSO 是地球上年际气候变率的主导模态。它涉及热带太平洋的海气相互作用，开尔文波/罗斯贝波、赤道上升流和 Bjerknes 反馈都在其中扮演关键角色。

📈 Progression logic · 递进逻辑：从最简单的地转平衡出发，逐步加入摩擦、层结、涡度、位涡守恒、波动、风生环流、西边界流，最终到海气耦合的 ENSO——每一步都在前一步的基础上增加一层物理现实。 Starting from the simplest geostrophic balance, we progressively add friction, stratification, vorticity, PV conservation, waves, wind-driven circulation, western boundary currents, and finally air–sea coupled ENSO — each step adds one more layer of physical reality on top of the previous.

⚡ 20% Core · 核心速览

The 20% That Gets You 80% · 五个核心抓手

Master these five, and you have the backbone of any wave-mechanics course · 掌握这五点，就抓住了波浪力学课程的主干

① Wave Properties · 波浪状态量

First identify wave height, period/frequency, wavelength/wavenumber, direction, and water depth. Classify as regular/irregular and deep/shallow before calculating anything. 先认清波高、周期/频率、波长/波数、传播方向与水深；先判定规则/不规则、深/浅水，再进入计算。

② Linear Waves & Dispersion · 线性波与色散

Understand the small-amplitude / potential-flow assumptions, boundary conditions, and the dispersion relation that links frequency, wavenumber, and depth. 理解小振幅/势流假设、边界条件及色散关系——它把频率、波数与水深联系起来。

③ Depth & Propagation · 深浅水与传播

Distinguish deep, intermediate, and shallow water. Know phase speed vs. group speed, and connect them to particle motion, pressure, and wave energy. 区分深水、过渡水深和浅水，分清相速与群速，并联系粒子运动、压力和波能。

④ Nearshore Processes · 近岸四过程

Shoaling, refraction, diffraction, and breaking — the four key transformations as waves approach the coast. The bridge from offshore theory to coastal engineering. 浅化、折射、绕射、破碎——是从外海波理论走向海岸过程的关键。

⑤ Statistical Description · 统计表达

Real seas are irregular — move from time-domain wave trains to frequency-domain wave spectra. Understand how the regular-wave model relates to statistical descriptions of real sea states. 真实海况不规则——从时域波列转到频域波谱，理解规则波模型与不规则海况统计描述的关系。

🌊 Sverdrup Balance · 位涡平衡

Wind-driven ocean circulation and the vorticity balance · 风生大洋环流与位涡平衡机制

🌪️ Sverdrup Balance: How the Wind Drives the Entire Ocean · 斯维尔德鲁普平衡：风如何驱动整片大洋

A three-step causal chain from wind stress to the great subtropical gyres — told through the classic schematic diagram. · 从风应力到巨型副热带涡旋的三步因果链——通过经典示意图娓娓道来。

The core question this diagram answers · 这幅图解答的核心问题：
How can a thin layer of wind at the sea surface drive the giant ocean gyres that span thousands of kilometers? The left half of the diagram paints the whole process in a 3-D sketch; the right half spells out the key logic in words. 海面上薄薄的风，凭什么能驱动横跨数千公里的巨型大洋环流？图左半部分用一张三维示意图把全过程画了出来，右半部分用文字点出关键逻辑。

📐 Layout of the diagram · 先看图的布局
The sea surface sits at the top, with two wind regimes labeled: the Westerlies to the north and the Trade Winds to the south, with red arrows marking "North" and "East" directions. Below the surface is a vertical section reaching down to the thermocline — the transition layer where temperature and density change sharply, and the lower boundary of the wind-driven layer. 海面在顶部，标着两个风向：偏北的是西风带，偏南的是信风带，分别用红色箭头标出"北"和"东"的方向。海面之下是垂直剖面，一直画到温跃层——海洋温度、密度急剧变化的过渡层，也是被风驱动水体的下边界。

🔗 The Core: A Three-Layer Causal Chain · 图的核心：一条三层因果链

① Wind makes surface water flow sideways · 第一层，风让表层水偏着流
In the Northern Hemisphere, under the influence of the Coriolis force, the surface water directly driven by the wind does not follow the wind direction — instead, the whole layer is deflected 90° to the right of the wind. This is the Ekman transport labeled in the diagram. As a result, water in the westerly belt deflects southward and water in the trade-wind belt deflects northward. The two converge in the middle of the subtropical ocean — matching the first key point on the right: "Wind causes Ekman transport and convergence." 北半球受科氏力影响，风直接带动的表层水不顺着风向走，而是整体偏向风向右侧 90 度，这就是图里标注的埃克曼输送。结果西风带的水向南偏、信风带的水向北偏，两股水在副热带海区中间汇合——对应右侧第一条要点"风造成埃克曼输送与辐合"。

② Convergence becomes a vertical "pump" · 第二层，辐合变成了垂直的"泵"
As water piles up in the middle, it can only be pushed downward. On the northern and southern sides, after surface water flows away, deeper water rises to replenish it. This downward pressing and upward pulling in the vertical direction is the Ekman pump. It squashes or stretches the entire water column from the sea surface down to the thermocline — matching the second key point on the right. 水在中部越堆越多，只能向下压；南北两侧表层水流走后，深层水向上补充。这个垂直方向的下压与上拉就是埃克曼泵，它把从海面到温跃层的整根水柱压扁或拉长——对应右侧第二条要点。

③ Water columns respond by "moving house" · 第三层，水柱靠"搬家"来响应
Rotating fluids obey potential vorticity conservation: the ratio of the Coriolis parameter to water-column thickness stays roughly constant, i.e. (f + ζ) / h ≈ constant. When the column thickness changes, the water cannot compensate by spinning faster in place — the diagram explicitly says "they don't spin up in situ". The only way out is to change latitude: squashed columns move toward the equator (where f is smaller), and stretched columns move toward the poles (where f is larger). This is exactly the pair of conclusions at the bottom of the diagram. 旋转流体遵循位涡守恒：科氏参数除以水柱厚度的比值大致不变，即 (f + ζ) / h ≈ 常数。水柱厚度被改变了，又无法靠原地加速旋转来弥补（图上明说"它们不会原地打转"），唯一出路是改变纬度——被压扁的水柱向赤道走，被拉长的水柱向极地走，这正是图底部那两行结论。

Squashing (h↓) → f must ↓ to conserve PV → equatorward movement · 压扁 → 向赤道移动
Stretching (h↑) → f must ↑ to conserve PV → poleward movement · 拉长 → 向极地移动

🌊 The big picture · 最终图景
Countless water columns drift slowly as a collective, ultimately forming the giant clockwise subtropical gyre shown on the top surface of the diagram, and producing the east-to-west Sverdrup transport. This is the engine behind strong currents like the Kuroshio and the Gulf Stream. 无数根水柱集体缓慢漂移，最终拼成图顶面那个顺时针的巨型副热带涡旋，并产生自东向西的斯维尔德鲁普输送——这就是黑潮、湾流等强洋流背后的发动机。

💡 记忆口诀 · Memory aid：风 → 埃克曼输送 → 辐合/辐散 → 埃克曼泵 → 水柱压扁/拉长 → 位涡守恒 → 改变纬度 → 斯维尔德鲁普输送 → 副热带涡旋。 Wind → Ekman transport → convergence/divergence → Ekman pumping → squashing/stretching → PV conservation → latitude change → Sverdrup transport → subtropical gyre.

Core Principle · 核心规律

Lower density ⇒ larger specific volume ⇒ higher dynamic height (sea surface)

🌀 Navier–Stokes Equations & Their Reductions · NS 方程及其退化

How the full momentum equation reduces to geostrophic, inertial and Ekman flow. · 完整动量方程在何种条件下退化为地转流、惯性流与埃克曼流。

① 完整 NS 动量方程 · The full momentum equation

In a rotating frame, per unit mass · 旋转坐标系下（单位质量）：

∂u/∂t + (u·∇)u + 2Ω × u = −(1/ρ)∇p + g + F

Horizontal components · 水平分量：

∂u/∂t + u·∇u − f v = −(1/ρ)·∂p/∂x + (1/ρ)·∂τx/∂z
∂v/∂t + u·∇v + f u = −(1/ρ)·∂p/∂y + (1/ρ)·∂τy/∂z

五项 · Five terms： ① 局地加速度 ∂u/∂t (local acceleration)　② 平流 / 惯性 (u·∇)u (advection)　③ 科里奥利 2Ω × u (Coriolis)　④ 压强梯度 (1/ρ)∇p (pressure gradient)　⑤ 摩擦 / 风应力 ∂τ/∂z (friction / wind stress)。 Which terms are kept and which are dropped defines each simplified flow below.

罗斯贝数 · Rossby number：Ro = U / (fL) 度量平流项与科里奥利项之比。Ro ≪ 1 ⇒ 平流可忽略 ⇒ 地转 / 埃克曼近似成立；Ro ≫ 1 ⇒ 平流主导 ⇒ 非地转（惯性流）。 Ro = U/(fL) measures advection against Coriolis. Ro ≪ 1 ⇒ advection negligible ⇒ geostrophic / Ekman; Ro ≫ 1 ⇒ advection dominates ⇒ ageostrophic (inertial).

G
Geostrophic flow · 地转流

Conditions · 条件：Ro ≪ 1（大尺度、缓变），定常 ∂/∂t = 0，无摩擦 F = 0，远离边界。 Large scale, steady, frictionless, away from boundaries.

Retained · 保留：科里奥利 + 压强梯度。Coriolis + pressure gradient.

−f vg = −(1/ρ)·∂p/∂x　　+ f ug = −(1/ρ)·∂p/∂y

Result · 结果：ug = −(1/ρf)·∂p/∂y，vg = (1/ρf)·∂p/∂x。沿等压线流动，北半球高压在右（见上方湾流算例）。 Flow along isobars, with high pressure on the right in the NH.

I
Inertial flow · 惯性流

Conditions · 条件：无压强梯度 ∇p ≈ 0，无摩擦，不受边界影响（孤立水质点）。 No pressure gradient, frictionless, isolated from boundaries.

Retained · 保留：局地加速度 + 科里奥利。Local acceleration + Coriolis.

∂u/∂t = f v　　∂v/∂t = −f u

Result · 结果：等速圆周运动（惯性振荡），周期 T = 2π/f，半径 R = V/f；北半球顺时针旋转。 Uniform circular motion (inertial oscillation): T = 2π/f, R = V/f; clockwise in the NH.

E
Ekman flow · 埃克曼流

Conditions · 条件：定常，f 平面，层内压强梯度可忽略，但海面存在风应力。 Steady, f-plane, PGF negligible inside the layer, but wind stress at the surface.

Retained · 保留：科里奥利 + 垂向应力散度。Coriolis + vertical stress divergence.

−f v = (1/ρ)·∂τx/∂z　　+ f u = (1/ρ)·∂τy/∂z

Result · 结果：埃克曼螺线；北半球表层流偏于风向右侧 45°，净输运偏右 90°：M = τ/(ρf)；抽吸 wE = curl(τ)/(ρf)。 Ekman spiral; NH surface current 45° right of the wind, net transport 90° right: M = τ/(ρf); pumping wE = curl(τ)/(ρf).
